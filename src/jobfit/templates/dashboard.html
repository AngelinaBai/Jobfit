ENGINEERING PORTFOLIO

How JobFit works

JobFit collects listings from documented public ATS APIs and public career pages, normalizes them into one schema, deduplicates rescans, and ranks opportunities against a personalized early-career profile.

IngestionConservative connectors with bounded timeouts and failure preservation.
MatchingExplainable scoring for role, skills, seniority, location, degree, and sponsorship signals.
OperationsFastAPI, PostgreSQL, scheduled scans, health checks, and idempotent migrations.
